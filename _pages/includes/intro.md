# 😸 About Me
I have received the Ph.D. degree in the [School of Computer Science and Engineering](https://cse.sysu.edu.cn) at [Sun Yat-sen University](https://www.sysu.edu.cn/sysuen) <img src='./images/sysu_logos_nobg.png' style='width: 6em;'> (中山大学计算机学院), supervised by [Prof. Xiaohua Xie (谢晓华教授)](https://cse.sysu.edu.cn/content/2478), and also co-supervised by [Prof. Jianhuang Lai (赖剑煌教授)](https://cse.sysu.edu.cn/content/2498).

My research interests focus on 3D/4D World Model, including reconstruction, understanding and simulation. I have published some papers at the top international CV journals and conferences, such as IJCV, IEEE TIP, IEEE TCSVT, ICCV and CVPR. I have served as a reviewer of IEEE TIP, IEEE TCSVT, CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, and ICME.
